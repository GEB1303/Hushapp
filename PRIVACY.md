# Hushapp Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

Hushapp is a Chrome extension that lets you blur or hide messages from specific members in WhatsApp Web group chats.

## Data we collect

**None.** Hushapp does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data.

## Data stored on your device

To work, Hushapp saves the following using Chrome's built-in storage (`chrome.storage.sync`):

- Your quiet list (the names or phone numbers you add)
- Your display preference (blurred or hidden)
- Whether the extension is turned on or off

This data stays in your browser. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome may sync these settings between your own devices through your Google account, as it does for any extension. Hushapp itself never sends this data to any server.

## Message content

Hushapp reads the sender name shown on WhatsApp Web messages, only inside your browser, to decide whether to blur or hide a message. Message content is never stored, copied, or transmitted.

## Permissions

- `storage`: to save your quiet list and preferences.
- Access to `web.whatsapp.com`: to apply the blur or hide effect on that page only.

## Third parties

Hushapp contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising, and does not communicate with any third-party service.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling Hushapp deletes all data it has stored.
