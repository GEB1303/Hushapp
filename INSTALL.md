# Installing Hushapp

Hushapp is not on the Chrome Web Store yet, so you install it manually. It takes about 2 minutes.

**Works on:** Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, and other Chromium-based browsers on a computer. It does not work on phones.

---

## Step 1 — Download

Click this link to download Hushapp:

**[⬇ Download Hushapp (ZIP)](https://github.com/GEB1303/Hushapp/archive/refs/heads/main.zip)**

## Step 2 — Unzip

1. Find the downloaded file, `Hushapp-main.zip`, in your Downloads folder.
2. Unzip it (on Windows: right-click → **Extract All**; on Mac: double-click).
3. You will get a folder named **`Hushapp-main`**.
4. Move that folder somewhere permanent, like your Documents folder.

> ⚠️ Don't delete or move this folder after installing. The browser loads Hushapp from it.

## Step 3 — Open the extensions page

Copy this address into your browser's address bar and press Enter:

| Browser | Address |
|---|---|
| Chrome | `chrome://extensions` |
| Edge | `edge://extensions` |
| Brave | `brave://extensions` |

## Step 4 — Turn on Developer mode

Turn on the **Developer mode** switch.
- Chrome and Brave: top right corner.
- Edge: left sidebar.

## Step 5 — Load Hushapp

1. Click **Load unpacked**.
2. Select the **`Hushapp-main`** folder (the one that contains `manifest.json`) and confirm.
3. Hushapp appears in your list of extensions. ✅

## Step 6 — Pin it and start using it

1. Click the puzzle-piece icon 🧩 in the browser toolbar and pin **Hushapp**.
2. Open or reload [WhatsApp Web](https://web.whatsapp.com).
3. Click the Hushapp icon, add members to your quiet list, and choose **Softly blurred** or **Hidden completely**.

---

## Updating to a new version

1. Download the ZIP again from the link in Step 1 and unzip it.
2. Replace the contents of your old `Hushapp-main` folder with the new files.
3. Go to your extensions page and click the **reload** icon ⟳ on the Hushapp card.

Your quiet list is kept.

## Uninstalling

Go to your extensions page and click **Remove** on the Hushapp card. You can then delete the folder.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Manifest file is missing or unreadable"**: you selected the wrong folder. Select the folder that directly contains `manifest.json`. Sometimes unzipping creates a folder inside another folder.
- **Messages aren't hidden**: reload WhatsApp Web, and check that the name in your quiet list matches exactly how it appears in the group. You can also use their phone number.
- **The browser shows a warning about developer mode extensions**: this is normal for extensions installed manually. You can dismiss it.

Still stuck? [Open an issue](https://github.com/GEB1303/Hushapp/issues).
